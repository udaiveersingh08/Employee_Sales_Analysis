# 📊 Sales Data Analysis & Business Performance Dashboard

> Sales Data Analysis Project using Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI.


## 📌 Project Overview

In this project, I analyzed sales data to understand business performance, product sales, profit, and city-wise sales.
I used Excel, Python (Pandas), SQL, and Power BI to clean the raw data, perform analysis, and create an interactive dashboard.
My main goal was to find the best-selling products, understand city-wise performance, and get useful business insights from the data.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare raw sales data.
- Find total sales, profit, and orders.
- Analyze product-wise sales performance.
- Compare sales across different cities.
- Identify the best-selling products.
- Understand business performance.
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🎯 Key Business Questions

1. What are the total sales and profit?
2. Which product generates the highest sales?
3. Which city has the highest sales?
4. How many orders were placed?
5. Which products have the lowest sales?
6. How can the business improve its sales performance?
